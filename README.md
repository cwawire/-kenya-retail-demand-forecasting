# Kenya Retail Demand Forecasting & Capacity Planning

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI project examining retail sales performance, customer demand patterns, inventory availability and workforce capacity requirements.

The project uses a simulated dataset representing an illustrative Nairobi retail store over a 90-day period.

## Business Objectives
- Analyse sales performance across retail product categories.
- Identify demand patterns and forecast short-term product demand.
- Evaluate stock coverage, reorder points and potential inventory shortages.
- Assess transaction volumes against available workforce capacity.
- Translate analytical findings into operational recommendations.

## Dashboard Modules

### 1. Retail Performance & Sales Analytics
- Units sold and sales revenue performance.
- Daily sales trends and 14-day demand forecast.
- Product-level sales performance.
- Stockout analysis.
- Transaction volumes and cashier availability.

### 2. Demand Forecasting & Inventory Planning
- Average daily demand.
- Current inventory levels.
- Stock coverage and supplier lead times.
- Reorder points and inventory risk.
- 14-day projected inventory exposure.

### 3. Workforce Capacity Planning
- Daily transaction trends.
- Cashier availability and attendance.
- Estimated staffing requirements.
- Workforce capacity gaps.
- Staffing shortage analysis and recommendations.

## Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data modelling
- Time-series forecasting
- Inventory analysis
- Workforce capacity analysis

## Data & Methodology
All sales, inventory, staffing and operational records are simulated for portfolio demonstration purposes. They do not represent actual supermarket data or actual Kenyan retail market performance.

## Key Learning Outcomes
The project demonstrates how business intelligence can connect commercial performance, demand forecasting, inventory management and operational capacity planning to support better business decisions.
